Using the diagram

Reading it
  • The area reads left to right: internet connections, then routers, then switches, then the devices on them.
  • A UPS sits under the server that reads it.
  • The layout is automatic.
Wires
  • A port shows only when a cable is drawn to it, on the side its cable leaves from.
  • Colors and line styles are in the Key, bottom left.
  • Cables to devices that are offline, unplaced, or on a port reported down are not drawn.
Patch panel
  • The row at the top holds one socket per cable leaving the area.
  • Hover, focus, or tap a socket to see where it goes, with a link to that area.
Details
  • Click a device for its details. Its own cables and every route to the gateway light up; the rest dim.
Moving around
  • Drag the background to pan, or scroll on a touch screen.
  • Zoom with the mouse wheel or + and −; Fit shows the whole area.
  • Wire style switches between curves and 90° bends.

Nothing here changes where equipment is placed.
