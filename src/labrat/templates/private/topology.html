Using the discovery map

Tiers follow observed cables: routers at the top, switches by hop, and equipment stacked under the device it connects to.

Hover or select a device to trace its path to the top. Drag the background to pan; drag a card to pin it, and click its pin (or double-click the card) to release it.

Line color is the cable medium and width its speed. Confirmed and inferred cables look the same (hover a line to see how it was found); only ambiguous links are drawn as tentative, and USB, relayed, and Wi-Fi relations have their own styles.

Wire style switches all paths between rounded and 90° bends. This browser remembers the Discovery choice separately from area Diagrams.

Highlight unplaced emphasizes equipment without a recorded placement. The map never changes a device's recorded location.
