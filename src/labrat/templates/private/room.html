Using the floor plan

Place equipment
  • Open Add equipment and drag a device onto the floor, a wall, or a shelf. Its toggles show infrastructure, clients, or only unplaced gear.
  • Drop it on a rack to set it on top.
  • Drop it on the Ceiling strip to mount it overhead.
Move or remove
  • Drag anything on the plan to move it.
  • Drag a device back onto the Add equipment list to take it out of the area.
Racks and shelves
  • Add them from Build out.
  • Drop a rack near a wall to stand it flush against it.
  • Hover a wall shelf and drag its end or front edge to resize it.
  • Use the pencil by a name to rename or delete.
Details
  • Click a device, or a row in a rack, to see its details.

Positions are relative, not to scale.
