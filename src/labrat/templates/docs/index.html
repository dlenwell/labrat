Know what's lurking in your lab

LabRat is a local-first discovery and physical inventory app for a homelab. A read-only collector reads your UniFi Network and Proxmox VE controllers, finds NUT UPS servers in the networks you allow it to scan, and can listen for MikroTik announcements. The web app shows what it found and where each piece of equipment lives: locations, areas, racks, shelves, ports, and cables.

How the pieces fit

  1. The collector observes. It runs on its own schedule, sends only read requests, and writes what it sees to a local cache. Browsing LabRat never contacts a controller.
  2. You decide what it means. A discovered device is an observation until an administrator places it or links it to an inventory record. LabRat never merges records or moves equipment on its own.
  3. The inventory is yours. Locations, areas, rack positions, and names you choose survive later scans, and the whole install can be exported and imported from Settings to move it.
